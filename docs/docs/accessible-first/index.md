## Who it is for

- **Use it for:** Public services, health, government and any product that must work for everyone.
- **Not for:** Dense expert tools where screen space matters more than size.
- **Products like:** Benefits application, Health booking, Public form, Council services
- **Themes:** light, dark

## Use scenarios

- A person with low vision zooms to 200% and applies for a benefit with a screen magnifier.
- A person with a tremor completes a form on a tablet.
