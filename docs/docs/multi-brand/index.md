## Who it is for

- **Use it for:** Companies that sell to customers under several brands, or white-label products shown to their clients' customers: sign-up, checkout, account and product pages.
- **Not for:** A single product with one brand, where the brand layer is cost with no user; or tools staff use all day. Use Dense data for those.
- **Products like:** Brand family of apps, White-label platform, Agency client portfolio
- **Themes:** aster-light, aster-dark, birch-light, birch-dark, coral-light, coral-dark

## Use scenarios

- A customer signs up for a plan on the Birch site, then manages it in the shared account area.
- A product team launches a fourth brand by adding brand primitives, without touching components.
