# The vision

A design system is a set of questions a team answers: who the product serves, what fills the screen, what people do, how text is set, how color carries meaning. Most systems keep the answers and lose the questions. Open DS keeps both.

## The aha

The question → answer → because mapping **is** the product. Tokens follow from the answers and are kept as reference. When you know why a token exists, you know what to change when the product changes.

## The layers

1. **Reasons**: business, people and work context, content and data, tasks and flows, human factors, outcomes, brand, voice and tone, team.
2. **Scopes**: typography, color, spacing, shape, elevation, motion, iconography, theming, accessibility, naming.
3. **Tokens**: primitives are the options, semantics are the decisions, themes are brands and modes.
4. **Component API**: one key per component element, variant and state. Every scheme fills every key.

## What a scheme delivers

- Tokens built with Style Dictionary: CSS variables for every brand in light and dark, a Tailwind and shadcn theme, DTCG JSON.
- Components copied into your project, the shadcn way. They read only component keys.
- Storybook, docs, usage rules and voice.
- An AI layer: `AGENTS.md`, an agent skill, and an MCP server.
